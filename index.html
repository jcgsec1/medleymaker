Medley Maker

Stitch lyric PDFs into instant medleys and sing song after song, nonstop.

Medley Maker is built for singers who perform many songs back to back. Pick songs from your library and the app stitches their lyric PDFs into one continuous medley in seconds. Drag to reorder, swap a song out, and you're ready for the stage.

Features

  • Instant medleys: combine any number of lyric PDFs into one smooth set
  • Stage-ready reader: a dark, glare-free view for dim stages
  • Tempo and key on every song: save the BPM and scale, and hear the tempo with one tap
  • Built-in Tanpura and Metronome for warm-up and practice
  • Folders and search to keep a big library in order
  • Cloud sync with Dropbox, Google Drive and folders on your phone
