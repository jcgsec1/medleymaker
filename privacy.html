Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Medley Maker ("the app") is made by Ritesh Gandhi. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: the app does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything stays on your phone or in your own cloud storage.

What the app stores

Your lyric PDFs, medleys, folders, tempo and key settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. We do not run any servers for the app and there are no Medley Maker accounts.

Dropbox and Google Drive

If you choose to connect Dropbox or Google Drive, you sign in on Dropbox's or Google's own sign-in page. The app never sees your password. The service gives the app an access token, which is kept in your device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).

The app uses that access only to:

  • show your folders so you can choose what to download or upload,
  • download the lyric PDFs you choose to your device,
  • upload the PDFs you choose from the app into the folder you choose.

Files travel directly between your device and Dropbox or Google Drive. They do not pass through us, and we cannot see them. The app does not use your Dropbox or Google data for anything else, does not share it with anyone, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Files on your phone

The app can read only the folders and PDFs you pick in your phone's own file picker. You can stop sharing any of them at any time from the app's Cloud sync screen.

Searching lyrics online

If you use the in-app browser to search for lyrics, those websites receive your request like any web browser's. Their own privacy policies apply.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is bought through Google Play (or the Apple App Store). The store handles the payment; the app never sees your card or payment details. The app only asks the store whether your account owns the unlock, so it can open. The 7-day free trial is counted on your device. There are no subscriptions.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Removing access and data

  • Disconnect Dropbox or Google Drive in the app (Cloud sync → Disconnect). This deletes the stored token.
  • You can also remove the app's access from your Dropbox connected apps or Google account connections.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
